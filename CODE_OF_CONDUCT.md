# Code of conduct

Beamline should be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute,
whatever your experience, background or language.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember that English or German may not
  be the other person's first language.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or sexual or violent content, here or in
  private messages to other contributors.

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers of
[LumpiLabs](https://github.com/LumpiLabs). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who keep breaking the rules.
