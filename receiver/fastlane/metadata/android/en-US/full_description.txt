Beamline TV turns a Fire TV or Android TV into a screen-mirroring receiver for Android phones with the Beamline app.

* Open Beamline on the TV, then tap the TV in the phone app or in its Quick Settings tile.
* On a Fire TV, a phone that has mirrored once can later start with the TV app closed: the TV wakes up and opens it by itself.
* Smooth, evenly paced playback with sound in sync. For 25 fps video the TV switches to 50 Hz, so every frame shows equally long.
* OK on the remote switches between filling the screen and showing the whole picture.
* Menu on the remote shows how mirroring is going: frame rate, delay behind the phone, Wi-Fi jitter, dropped frames and sound gaps.
* Encrypted, and only for phones you allowed: the first time, the TV and the phone show the same code, and you press OK.
* In English and German.
* No ads, accounts, subscriptions or tracking. Free software under the GPLv3.

Runs on Fire TV with Fire OS 6 or newer and on Android TV 7.1 or newer.
