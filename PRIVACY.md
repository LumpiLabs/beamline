# Privacy

Beamline collects nothing about you.

- No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- The phone app talks only to the TV you pick, on your local network. The TV app talks
  only to phones on your local network. Neither app contacts any server on the
  internet.
- The screen and sound you mirror go straight from the phone to the TV and are not
  stored anywhere.
- The phone app captures only what other apps play, never the microphone. Android
  calls the permission for this "record audio".
- Each app keeps a few settings on the device (for example the last TV used), its own
  key for encrypting the connection, and the keys' fingerprints of the devices it was
  paired with. They stay on the device and are left out of backups.
