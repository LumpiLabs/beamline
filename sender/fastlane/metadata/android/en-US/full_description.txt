Beamline mirrors your Android phone's screen, with sound, to a Fire TV or Android TV on the same Wi-Fi that runs the Beamline TV app.

* One tap in the app or in Quick Settings starts and stops mirroring.
* Sound from your apps plays on the TV, in sync with the picture. Beamline only captures what apps play, never the microphone.
* Smooth playback comes first: the TV keeps about a third of a second in hand, which hides short Wi-Fi hiccups.
* After a Wi-Fi drop, mirroring picks up again by itself.
* The phone stays awake, dimmed, while mirroring, because Android stops sending the picture once the phone locks. A switch turns this off.
* In English and German.
* No ads, accounts, subscriptions or tracking. Free software under the GPLv3.

Copy-protected video (Netflix, Disney+ and similar) shows black when mirrored, as with every mirroring app. The phone's built-in Cast button can't target Beamline: start from the Beamline app or its Quick Settings tile.

Needs Android 10 or newer, and the Beamline TV app on the TV.
